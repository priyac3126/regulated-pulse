The Pain Brokers

Reading List · July 30, 2026

Released Jan 2026.

The Pain Brokers was a fantastic read intersecting the worlds of legal and business corruption, women systematically being taken advantage of, and the chilling effects of the lack of the medical device industry can have on thousands of Americans.

In other words, it is a must-read to gain a deeper understanding into how mass torts, filed as hundreds or thousands of individual lawsuits, are orchestrated across a cross-country, multi-year process to expose defective healthcare technology, in this case, about pelvic mesh. Several corporations and bad actors took advantage of women by telling them their pelvic mesh was a “ticking time bomb” and they would have to seek removal surgery in a few select locations, primarily, in Florida.

As readers turn every page, they become ever-more horrified by the injustices that women underwent in the 21st century, revealing how cracks in medical device regulation can lead to lifelong injury and disability for women that simply took the advice of their doctors to have pelvic mesh input to treat pelvic organ prolapse and reduce incontinence.
