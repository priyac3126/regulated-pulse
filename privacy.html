1. Scope

This policy applies to theregulatedpulse.com, its articles, podcast pages, comment features, newsletter sign-up, and related services operated by The Regulated Pulse. It does not apply to third-party websites, podcast platforms, or services that we link to or embed.

The Regulated Pulse is an independent educational publication operated by Priya Chaudhari in Georgia, United States.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact and inquiry information: your name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to include when you contact us or ask about being a podcast guest.
  • Comments and contributions: the content of comments, questions, or other material you submit, along with the email address or account information needed to manage your submission.
  • Newsletter information: your email address and related subscription details when you sign up through our newsletter form.
  • Account information: your email address, authentication provider, and account identifiers when you create an account or sign in by email link, Google, or Apple.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and device information: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, requested pages, and timestamps. These data may appear in standard hosting, security, and service-provider logs.
  • Usage information: pages, episodes, or features you interact with, including likes and view history stored on your device. Some usage information may be linked to your account if you are signed in.
  • Cookies and local storage: small data files and browser storage used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, protect the site, and understand which content you have already viewed.

Information from sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google or Apple, the provider may share basic account information with our authentication provider, such as your email address, display name, and provider-specific account identifier. We do not receive your provider password.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • operate, maintain, and secure the website;
  • authenticate users and manage accounts;
  • publish and moderate comments and contributions;
  • respond to questions, guest inquiries, and other messages;
  • send newsletter updates to people who ask to receive them;
  • remember accessibility, theme, playback, and reading preferences;
  • personalize content recommendations and view history on your device;
  • monitor site performance, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions; and
  • improve the site's content, features, and accessibility.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for targeted advertising on this site.

4. Legal bases

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Consent: for optional preference storage, newsletter communications, and any optional features that ask for your permission.
  • Contract or requested service: to provide an account, publish a comment you submit, or respond to your request.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, and improve the site, prevent abuse, and communicate about content and inquiries.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

6. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host, secure, authenticate, store data, deliver email, or support the site, including Google Firebase and MailerLite;
  • Podcast and video platforms when you choose to play or follow content through YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, Pocket Casts, or similar services;
  • Professional advisers such as legal, accounting, or security providers when reasonably necessary;
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, to protect rights or safety, to investigate abuse, or to enforce our terms; and
  • A successor organization in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of the site, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Third-party services

The site uses or links to third-party services that have their own privacy practices, including:

  • Google Firebase: authentication and database services for accounts and comments.
  • MailerLite: newsletter sign-up and email delivery.
  • Hosting and delivery networks: GitHub Pages, content delivery networks, Google Fonts, and script libraries used to serve the site.
  • Embedded media and podcast platforms: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, Pocket Casts, and similar services.

These providers may process technical information such as your IP address, device information, and interaction data under their own policies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before using their features.

8. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to operate the site, maintain accounts, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and the context in which it was collected.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your email account and sign-in credentials secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent;
  • request a portable copy of information you provided; and
  • appeal a decision we make about your privacy request.

To exercise a right, contact hello@theregulatedpulse.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. If you are a California resident, you may request information about the categories of personal information we collect, disclose, or share, and request deletion or correction, as provided by applicable law.

10. Children's privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

If you are between 13 and the age of majority in your jurisdiction, please use the site only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

11. International transfers

The site is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be highlighted on the site. Your continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
