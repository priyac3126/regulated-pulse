1. Scope

This policy applies to theregulatedpulse.com, including its articles, podcast pages, comments, newsletter sign-up, and related services. It does not apply to third-party websites, podcast platforms, or services we link to or embed.

The site is operated by Priya Chaudhari in Georgia, United States.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, message, comments, newsletter details, or other information you choose to submit.
  • Account information: your email address, authentication provider, and account identifiers when you sign in by email link, Google, or Apple.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser, device, requested pages, referring page, and timestamps in standard hosting and security logs.
  • Usage and preferences: pages or episodes you interact with, likes, view history, and accessibility or display settings, some of which are stored on your device or linked to your account when signed in.
  • Cookies and local storage: small browser-stored data used for sign-in, security, preferences, and your cookie choice.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure the site, authenticate users, publish and moderate comments, respond to inquiries, send newsletter updates to subscribers, remember preferences, personalize recommendations, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, comply with legal obligations, and improve the site.

Where required, we rely on consent, a requested service, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the site, or a legal obligation. You may withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Cookies and storage

We ask for your cookie choice on your first visit and store that choice so we can honor it. If you allow cookies, we also remember display and reading preferences. If you choose Essential only or No cookies, optional and functional storage is limited as described in the banner. You can change your choice at any time using .

We do not use advertising cookies. Third-party services may set their own cookies when you use them, and your browser settings can block or delete cookies.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed with service providers that host, secure, authenticate, store data, deliver email, or support the site, including Google Firebase and MailerLite. MailerLite's newsletter script is loaded only if you allow optional cookies.

We may also share information with podcast or video platforms when you choose to use them, professional advisers when necessary, authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety, or a successor organization in a merger or transfer. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Third-party services include Google Firebase, MailerLite, GitHub Pages and delivery networks, Google Fonts, and embedded platforms such as YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music, and Pocket Casts. They process technical data under their own policies.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a privacy decision. To make a request, contact theregulatedpulse@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

EEA and UK residents may also complain to their local data-protection authority. California residents may request information about the categories of personal information collected, disclosed, or shared, and may request deletion or correction as provided by law.

8. Children and international transfers

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can address it.

We operate from the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy by revising the effective date above. Material changes may also be highlighted on the site. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to theregulatedpulse@gmail.com. For podcast inquiries, contact Theregulatedpulsepodcast@gmail.com.
